    Opening Knov on a Mac for the first time
    
      
      The Mac version isn't signed by Apple yet, so macOS blocks it the first time. Unzip it, drag Knov into Applications, then open it once with Control-click → Open. If macOS says the app is damaged, open Terminal, paste the line below and press Return:
    
      
    
      xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Knov.app
      
  
      
  
      
    You'll also need a Claude (Pro or Max) or ChatGPT (Plus or higher) account. Knov installs the AI programs for you.
  
      

      

      
  
      Questions
      
  
      
    Do I need to know how to code?
    
      No. You write requests the way you'd ask a person, and approve or decline with buttons. Knov never shows you a terminal.
      
  
      
  
      
    Is Knov free?
    
      Yes. The AI itself runs on your own Claude or ChatGPT subscription, so its usage limits apply.
      
  
      
  
      
    Where do my files go?
    
      Knov runs on your computer and keeps its records there. The AI agents read the files they work on and send that content to their provider (Anthropic or OpenAI), just as they do when you use them directly.
      
  
      
  
      
    What if the AI breaks something?
    
      Undo the request. Knov puts the folder's files back the way they were and moves anything new to a safe box instead of deleting it.
      
  
      
  
      
    Which AI agents does it support?
    
      Claude Code (Anthropic) and Codex (OpenAI). You can use both, side by side.
